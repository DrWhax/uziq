Installing Uziq
===============

1. Drag Uziq into the Applications folder.
2. In Applications, Control-click Uziq and choose Open.
3. Confirm Open in the security prompt.

The extra first-launch step is required because this family build is ad-hoc
signed rather than notarized with an Apple Developer ID certificate. After the
first approval, Uziq can be opened normally from Applications or the Dock.

Uziq requires macOS 15 or newer. This package includes its Spotify playback
helper; no separate librespot installation or binary selection is required.
